some resources

  • Art of Problem Solving (the math bible)
  • INTEGIRLS (math contests and opportunities for girls / non-binary individuals)
    • INTEGIRLS Bay Area (the chapter i ran!). you can participate in our events even if you reside outside of the bay.
  • Athemath (math classes for girls by girls)
  • Math Prize for Girls (prestigious math contest at MIT for girls / non-binary individuals)
  • Canadian Open Math Challenge (COMC) (i think this competition is a really fun alternative to the u.s.-based amcs, gets you into proofs!)
  • Non-Trivial Fellowship (research program w/ funding)
  • PROMYS (6-week number theory summer program)
  • Euler Circle (advanced math classes)
  • other websites: quanta magazine, An Infinitely Large Napkin, 3blue1brown, elvissaravia on substack, andrew ng, poesie poetry app, mit ocw
  • advice: become unapologetically yourself. no one has everything figured out anyways.
